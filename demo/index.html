Power Loader demo pages

Fictional records from a fictional council case system, for trying out the extension.

Set up Power Loader for this demo

  1. Install Power Loader in Firefox on a desktop computer and open its sidebar with the toolbar button.
  2. On the Case tab, under Connect your team’s forms library, enter this library address and press Connect:
    https://powerloader.surprisingly.ltd/demo/library/
  3. When Firefox asks whether Power Loader may access this site, choose Allow. The sidebar then lists the demo flows: start one to begin a case.

Already using another library? Change the address under Settings → Forms library instead. The library manifest lists every form and profile with its checksum.

New to Power Loader? The demo guide walks through these pages step by step.

The casework systems: 100 cases

Five fictional places with 100 cases and the people behind them, generated from a fixed seed (tools/build-demo-systems.mjs). Everything in them is made up. Start at a system’s homepage, as a caseworker would:

  • Case Manager: the council’s case system. My work, the work queue of 100 cases and a page per case. Mark a case done on its case page; done cases are kept in this browser only, and reset the demo starts again.
  • Benefits and Income Verification: employment and income for each person, listed by surname.
  • Harbour Homes: the housing association’s partner portal, a rent account for each tenancy.
  • Customer Messages: the Housing Support Team’s inbox. Each client has six messages; only some online forms (Tell us about a change) carry an effective date, a new weekly income or a tenancy end date, and the list doesn’t say which. Partner organisations send referrals, urgent or routine, open or already picked up, and emails asking about them, all with the subject Referral received: only opening each one tells you which are urgent and open. The messages are also served as data, for example NC-5347.json.
  • Staff intranet: the council’s own procedure for a rent support review, with the checks for every case you might meet.

A few cases don’t agree across systems: case 1003’s rent account has an old address, case 1007’s income record spells the surname differently, and case 1005’s income record is a different person under the same reference. The form is Rent support review (open it from a case so it knows which one).

Try it by hand first

Open the team’s rent support review procedure and work one case by hand, as a new caseworker would: note the time you start, follow the steps through every system, and note the time you finish. Then do another case with the Rent support review flow and compare.

The rent support review flow

With the demo library connected, the flow is already listed on the Case tab; without it, download Rent support review.flow.json from this copy of the demo, so it points at these pages, and open it in Build with Flows → Import a flow…. Start a case: there’s no reference to type. Go to the work queue and open the case you’re picking up, and Power Loader takes the case reference from its address. Clip the case, the rent account and the income record; on the client’s messages one fetch step reads all six message pages, 4 at a time, and clips the change from the one that has it. Then it fills the review and opens an email to the income team.

If the case had no tenancy reference, the step Ask the client for a missing reference opens an email to the client, written from the case: their name, the case reference and the words the team always uses, ready to check and send. It takes a click instead of a few minutes’ typing, and every client gets the same clear request.

The demo library has a site rule for reading pages in the background: fetching is allowed only for the messages service; try a fetch step elsewhere to see the library’s rule refuse it. Settings → Sites Power Loader works on shows the rule, locked by the library. A second flow, Rent support review (messages as data), fetches the same messages as one JSON response instead.

Triage the team inbox

The Inbox triage flow fills no form. Start it, open the team inbox and press Fetch 25 messages: Power Loader reads every message on the page in the background and lists the 4 urgent referrals nobody has picked up, marked in the inbox too. Two of them are for cases 1012 and 1020: finish the triage, then start a rent support review for one of them.

Make a flow (no library needed)

A flow is what a library is made of: the form a case fills, the pages it clips from, and the fields between them. You can make one here without connecting the demo library.

  1. In the Power Loader sidebar, open Flows and choose New flow (or Or make a flow → New flow on the Case tab).
  2. Name it, and paste the guidance below into Guidance.
  3. Open Form to fill 1, go to the Discretionary housing payment form in the same tab and choose Use this form. Then choose ✦ Make the template with AI (needs ✦ Remote AI in Settings) or Make it by hand: type a field’s name and click its box.
  4. Open Page to clip 1 and use the client record. Add two more pages to clip for the rent account and employment and income, and do the same on each.
  5. Test each page. Test the client record on the other client too, then go back to the flow, fix anything under Checks, and choose Export.
  6. Optional: under 3. Pages to visit, add one and use My cases. Then, under The caseworker’s steps, put it first and choose ✦ Write the steps from the guidance, or edit each step: what to do there, and links to the next page. A link can carry a value from the case, such as client-{ref}.html.
  7. Optional: under Timelines, add one with bars for employments (rows of the history table on the employment page: pick a date in it) and a pointer for the tenancy start date. A case from the flow draws it on the Tools tab.
