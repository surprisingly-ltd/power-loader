From carrying files to systems that share them.

Power Loader today (chapters 1–5). It turns the copying you do by hand into a flow: a reviewable list of what to copy from each page and which box it goes in, carried in one bookmark. Show it each page once. After that it copies when you press Copy and fills when you press Fill. It never submits the form for you.

Where this is heading (chapters 6–8). Fully automated processes that run the learned routes without anyone pressing a button, at fleet scale, and finally underlying systems fixed so they share data directly, with nothing left to copy.
