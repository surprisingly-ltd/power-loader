# no-remote-ai

Turns the remote AI off and locks it off.

**What it does**
- `gateway.enabled: false`. No ✦ action sends anything. Settings says the organisation has turned the remote AI off.

**What it leaves open**
- Everything on this computer still works: reading pages, filling forms, the on-device privacy model and OCR.
- Sites, the library and the models are as the public build.
