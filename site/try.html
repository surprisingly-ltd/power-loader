Training · Level 2

Try a flow

Load a ready-made flow and run a whole case with it: clip a client’s details from three fictional systems, settle a conflict, catch the wrong person, and fill a benefit form. It takes about ten minutes.

Everything under /demo/ is made up: Northshire Council, its clients, its landlord and its forms. The demo pages store nothing, but submitting the demo DHP form puts your answers in the confirmation page’s address, which reaches the web host like any page request. Use only made-up details.

Finish Level 1 first, so the add-on is installed. You don’t need the AI gateway or the privacy model for this level.

1. Load a flow

A flow is the form a case fills, the pages its answers come from, and which value goes where. There are two ways to get one. Teams normally use a forms library. A single flow file is handy for trying something out or passing a flow on to someone else.

Option A: connect the demo forms library

  1. In the sidebar, open Settings. Under Forms library, enter this Library address:
    {{DEMO_LIBRARY}}
  2. Leave Branch as main and press Connect.
  3. Firefox asks whether Power Loader may access {{HOST}}. Choose Allow. Power Loader downloads the library, checks every file against its manifest and lists four forms.

The library’s flows are then already listed on the Case tab, ready to start, and in Build (the switch at the top of the sidebar) on the Flows tab, under From your library: you don’t need to download them. They’re read-only there; Make a copy to edit puts one on your computer to change.

Option B: import a flow file

  1. Download a flow:
    Discretionary housing payment.flow.json Housing support referral.flow.json Rent support review.flow.json Rent support review (messages as data).flow.json Inbox triage.flow.json
  2. In the sidebar, switch to Build and press Import a flow… on the Flows tab. Choose the file you downloaded.
  3. The flow appears in the list, marked with problems: hasn’t been tested on its page. That’s expected, and it doesn’t stop you starting a case. A flow’s pages are always tested on the computer that uses them. Level 3 shows you how.

The files are plain JSON. Open one to see what a flow is: the fields, each page’s address pattern, and for each field the element it’s read from or the box it goes into. They were made from the demo library, so options A and B give you the same flow.

2. Start a case

  • Every case is a run of a flow. On the Case tab, the flows from your library and on this computer are listed together: press Start next to Discretionary housing payment. Star the flows you work and they come first. Power Loader says which pages haven’t been tested on this computer; press Start case again to carry on. A flow that starts by choosing a case, such as the rent support review, needs no case reference: open the case in the work queue and Power Loader takes its number.

The case lists every field the form needs, all empty for now.

3. Clip the client’s details

  1. Open Client NC-1042: Maria Kowalska, the council’s case system. The first time, the sidebar asks for access to this site: press Allow on {{HOST}}.
  2. The sidebar lists every step of the flow, and opens the one for this page. Its green button is always the next thing to do: here, Clip this page. Her name, date of birth, NI number and address fill the case, each linked to where it came from. Then the green button is Next, to the next step’s page.
  3. Open her rent account at Harbour Homes and clip it.
  4. Clip employment and income too. It spells her surname differently, so the sidebar shows 1 conflict to decide. Press Use this next to the one you trust.

4. Catch the wrong person

  1. Open Client NC-2077: David Okafor, a different person, and press Clip this page.
  2. The sidebar warns This may be a different person before anything else.
  3. Press Undo this clip, keep Wrong person selected and press Remove clip. Everything that came from that clip is cleared.

5. Fill the form

  1. Open the Discretionary housing payment form and press Fill this form in the sidebar.
  2. A fill plan lists what will go in each box, what’s missing and what stays empty. Nothing touches the page yet. Press Fill n boxes.
  3. Type the reason for the application yourself (it isn’t on any page), check the form and submit it. Power Loader never submits. The confirmation page is recognised, and its reference is recorded in the case.
  4. Started with the referral too? Fill the Housing support referral the same way. The values you’ve already checked are reused.

6. Finish the case

  1. Press Finish case. It lists what was filled and what wasn’t.
  2. Tick the confirmation and press Finish and delete data. The case and its values are deleted from Firefox. The receipt that remains names no one.

Try it by hand first

The fictional council has its own procedure for a rent support review on its staff intranet. Work one case by hand with it, as a new caseworker would, and note the time you start and finish. Then do the next case with the flow below and compare.

A bigger case: the rent support review

The work queue has 100 cases. Start a case from Rent support review (listed from the library, or imported above). There’s no case reference to type: its first step, Choose a case, sends you to the work queue, and when you open a case Power Loader takes the case reference from its address. Then the flow takes you to the case, its rent account and income record, then to the client’s messages. There one fetch step reads all six messages in the background, 4 at a time, and clips a change of circumstances from whichever online form has it. The demo library allows fetching only from the messages service, so the same step pointed at another system is refused by the library’s rule.

If a case has no tenancy reference, the step Ask the client for a missing reference opens an email to the client in your email app, already written from the case with their name and the case reference. Writing it from a template takes one click rather than several minutes, and the wording is the same every time. When the review is submitted, the flow’s last email tells the income team; mark the case done on its case page and pick up the next one.

Triage the team inbox

The team inbox doesn’t say which messages are referrals, how urgent they are, or whether anyone has picked them up. Start a case from Inbox triage (from the library, or download it): it fills no form. On the inbox, press Fetch 25 messages. Power Loader reads every message on the page in the background and lists the 4 open urgent referrals in the sidebar, marked in the inbox too. Open one, finish the triage, then start the rent support review for its client: two of them, cases 1012 and 1020, are in the work queue.

Running the demo on your own computer

From the source, run pnpm install then pnpm demo, and use the library address http://localhost:8787/demo/library/. The pages are the same.
